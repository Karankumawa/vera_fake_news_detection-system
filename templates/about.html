About The Project & Author

Vera of articles (formerly Fake News Detection) is a machine learning project designed to identify when a news source may be producing fake news. The core classification engine uses a Passive Aggressive Classifier and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze article text and determine its authenticity with high accuracy.
